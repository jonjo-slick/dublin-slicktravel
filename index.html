Private Aviation

Depart via private jet from the Harrods Private Terminal, Stansted. Skip the commercial chaos and enjoy a seamless, VIP transit into the heart of Dublin.

Hosted by a Legend

Join Geordan Murphy for the weekend. Capped 72 times for Ireland, a Six Nations Grand Slam winner in 2009, and a British & Irish Lions tourist, Geordan spent 16 years at the top of the club and international game with Leicester Tigers. He's on hand throughout the weekend to share the stories and insight from a career most fans only see from the stands.

Luxury Accommodation

Enjoy our stay at The Dean Dublin, ideally located 600m from St Stephen's Green. Featuring a state-of-the-art gym, sauna, and heated relaxation pool.
